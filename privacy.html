Privacy Policy

How FlagForge handles your data.

FlagForge collects minimal data required for account management and scoring. No personal data is shared without your consent.

Usage data is stored in Firebase Firestore for scoreboard and analytics purposes. All passwords are secured through Firebase Auth with industry-standard hashing.

Challenge submissions, solve times, and flags are stored to operate the competition and are visible to other participants on the leaderboard.

Contact us at support@flagforge.example to request data deletion.
