# Competition States

Solved:
A resolved/complete visual state without relying on a generic checkmark.

Unsolved:
Neutral and inviting, not broken or disabled.

Locked:
Clearly obstructed, with a readable reason.

First Blood:
Rare, strong, restrained, dedicated SVG treatment.

Ranks:
1st/2nd/3rd should have distinct hierarchy without generic trophy emoji.

Loading:
Prefer structural/progress indicators over a generic spinner when practical.

Error:
Clear and restrained. Do not turn the whole page red.
