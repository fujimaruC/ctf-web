Rules:
1. Amber is the brand identity.
2. Semantic colors stay secondary.
3. Avoid gradients as the default treatment.
4. Avoid pervasive glow.
5. Never use neon green just to signal cybersecurity.
6. Prefer a small number of meaningful colors over decorative color variety.
